Account & Data Deletion

Zombie Wars · 5th Table

Before you delete

Deleting your account permanently removes everything tied to it:

  • Your player profile and display name
  • All progress — unlocked maps, vehicles, weapons and upgrades
  • All in-game currency and unclaimed mail rewards
  • Your leaderboard entries

Deletion cannot be undone. Anything you purchased is removed with the account and cannot be restored afterwards, so use any remaining currency first.

The fastest way is inside the game — it takes effect immediately:

Profile → Edit profile → Delete account

If you can no longer open the game on your device, you can send us a deletion request by email instead. If the game still opens, copy your Player ID first — it is shown in Profile → Edit profile below the Delete account button (tap the ID to copy it) and lets us find your account exactly.

Confirm your request

We will reply to your email to verify the request before anything is deleted — game accounts are not linked to an email address, so this check protects your account from deletion requests made by someone else.

Once verified, deletion is permanent.
